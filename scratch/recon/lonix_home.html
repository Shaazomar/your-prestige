Welcome To Lonix

Your trusted partner in manufacturing and
exporting the best Vitrified Tiles.

Lonix is a leading manufacturer and exporter of ceramic tiles founded in 2021 by Mr. Bharat Dalsaniya, a specialist and passionate person. Lonix Ceramic, the most skilled company in Morbi—the tiles hub of India—offers a stunning collection of Large-format Glazed Vitrified Tiles and Porcelain Glazed Vitrified Tiles that elevate your space with gorgeous designs and durability. As a company committed to quality, Lonix continues to raise the bar in innovation and excellence in tile manufacturing.

Lonix specializes in the production of porcelain tiles. We stand out in the global market because we are experts in manufacturing vitrified tiles. Our tiles perform well in diverse spaces because they are useful and stylish. This makes them great for residential and commercial areas.

At Lonix, we think that tiles are more than just useful surfaces; they're also a canvas for design. Our tiles have the best design and longevity in the world. Our cutting-edge technology makes us one of the most trusted names in the tile industry.
